## What are your support hours?
> When are you open?
> Are you available on weekends?
Our support team is online Monday to Friday, 9am to 6pm US Eastern. This assistant answers 24/7, and it can hand you to a person during those hours.

## How much does hosting cost?
> What are your prices?
> How much is a plan?
Plans start at $9/month for the Starter plan (1 site, 10 GB SSD). The Business plan is $29/month (10 sites, 50 GB SSD, daily backups). Annual billing saves two months.

## Do you offer a free trial or refund?
> Can I get my money back?
Every plan has a 30-day money-back guarantee. There is no separate free trial; cancel inside 30 days for a full refund.

## How do I reset my password?
> I can't log in to my account
Go to the login page and click "Forgot password". We email a reset link that is valid for 30 minutes. If it doesn't arrive, check spam or ask here to talk to a person.

## Do you include SSL certificates?
Yes. Every site gets a free Let's Encrypt SSL certificate that is issued and renewed automatically.

## How do backups work?
The Business plan includes automatic daily backups kept for 14 days. You can restore any backup from the dashboard in one click. Starter plans can buy backups as a $3/month add-on.

## Can you migrate my existing website?
> Can you move my site from another host?
> I want to transfer my website to you
Yes. We migrate one website for free on any plan. Send us your old host login through the dashboard and we usually finish within 24 hours with no downtime.

## Which payment methods do you accept?
> What cards do you take?
> Can I pay with PayPal?
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express and PayPal. Business customers can ask for invoice billing.

## How do I point my domain to you?
> How do I change my DNS or nameservers?
Change your domain's nameservers to ns1.acme.example and ns2.acme.example at your registrar. DNS changes can take up to 24 hours to spread.
